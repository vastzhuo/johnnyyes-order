Returns, Refunds & Delivery Policy

Johnny Yes (johnnyyes.com.au; online order site). Updated 28 November 2026. Contact: info@johnnyyes.com.au.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL) cannot be excluded by this policy or anything we say or do. This is not legal advice.

Consumer guarantees

Goods we sell have automatic ACL guarantees of acceptable quality, matching description, and fitness for any particular purpose you made known to us or that we represented. If a guarantee is not met, you are entitled to a remedy. We may assess the product first, and we will not use a “no refunds” statement to deny an ACL remedy.

If the failure is major (unsafe, very different from its description, a problem that would have stopped someone buying it, or cannot be used as intended and cannot easily be fixed), you may choose a full refund or a same-type replacement, with no deduction for use. If it is minor, we will repair or fix it free within a reasonable time, or we may offer a replacement or refund. If we cannot or will not, you may get a refund or replacement, or have it fixed elsewhere and recover the reasonable cost. A remedy is generally not available if you changed your mind, misused the product, or were told about the problem before buying.

Trade and business purchases

Trade purchases may also be covered by the ACL. Goods bought for business use are generally covered if they cost less than $100,000 including GST, or are of a kind commonly bought for personal, domestic or household use. Guarantees generally do not apply if goods are bought for resupply, use or transformation in production or manufacturing, or to repair or treat other goods.

Change of mind

We do not offer change-of-mind refunds or exchanges. This does not affect your rights for faulty, damaged or incorrect goods.

Returns, damaged goods and freight

Email info@johnnyyes.com.au promptly with your name, order details, the problem, and photos of any damage or incorrect items. We may ask for proof of purchase; original packaging is not required for ACL returns.

You return goods that can be easily posted. If they are large, heavy or hard to return, we collect them or pay shipping within a reasonable time. If we confirm a problem, we reimburse reasonable return costs already paid (keep receipts). If there is no problem, we may charge reasonable collection and inspection costs only if we estimated them before collection.

Refunds

Refunds go to your nominated Australian bank account, or in the same form as your original payment if you prefer (for example, cash if you paid cash on delivery). We do not take card payments online.

Delivery

We deliver only, to the Melbourne metro area — no pickup. Delivery costs, timing and the exact area are confirmed when we confirm your order. If a problem cannot be resolved, contact your state or territory consumer protection agency, or report it to the ACCC.
