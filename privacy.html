Privacy Policy

Johnny Yes (johnnyyes.com.au; online order site). Updated 28 November 2026.

This policy explains how Johnny Yes handles personal information. It is aligned with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), in a small-business-appropriate way. The Privacy Act does not cover every small business — this depends on annual turnover and certain exceptions. We still aim to handle your information carefully and in line with this policy. This is not legal advice.

Who we are

Johnny Yes is a Melbourne business selling tyre repair consumables, workshop tools and wheel protection products to retail and trade customers. Email: info@johnnyyes.com.au.

What we collect

When you order on our order site or request a trade quote on johnnyyes.com.au, we collect the name, business name, phone, email, delivery address and suburb, notes, and business details you provide. Our hosts may log limited technical data (such as IP address and pages visited). We do not collect card details online. Payment is offline only (bank transfer, cash on delivery, or pay on invoice).

Why we collect it

We use this information to process and fulfil orders and quotes, deliver goods in Melbourne metro, contact you about your order, quote, delivery or a return, and meet legal record-keeping obligations. We only collect what is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • FormSubmit, which emails online orders to info@johnnyyes.com.au
  • our email and hosting providers, including GitHub Pages (United States) and VentraIP (Australia)
  • a delivery provider, to deliver your order
  • advisers or a government agency, if the law requires it

Personal information may be disclosed to, or stored by, overseas recipients. Where practicable, we identify that GitHub is in the United States. We have not independently confirmed FormSubmit’s server locations.

Storage

Orders and quotes arrive by email and are kept in our business records. We take reasonable steps to protect them from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. We keep them only as long as needed to fulfil orders, handle enquiries and meet legal obligations.

Access and correction

To access or correct your personal information, email info@johnnyyes.com.au. We will respond within 5 business days.

Marketing

We do not use your information for marketing unless you opt in.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, email info@johnnyyes.com.au with what happened and how you would like it resolved. We will respond within 5 business days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). The OAIC generally expects you to complain to us first and give us at least 30 days to respond. See Complain to an organisation or agency and Privacy complaints.
